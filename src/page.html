MANIC ANTEATER

10 rooms. Every ant hill. One portal.

←→ walk SPACE or ↑ jump ENTER start P pause N warp Gamepads work too

Jumps commit you to an arc, and walking off a ledge drops you straight down. Fall more than eight tiles and you won't get up again. Your energy drains all the time, and whatever's left when you reach the portal is added to your score. After the tenth room the game loops with faster beasts. Stuck on a room? Double-click the level name (or press N) to warp to the next one. Install it as an app to play full screen and offline.
